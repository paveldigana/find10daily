Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the website find10daily and “find10daily uploader”, a private tool that publishes find10daily videos to the find10daily accounts on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

What we collect

Nothing from visitors or viewers. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. The uploader is used only by the owner of the find10daily accounts; it does not access anyone else’s account or data.

Data from Google / YouTube and TikTok

The uploader asks only for permission to upload videos to the owner’s own channel (YouTube scope youtube.upload; TikTok scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). It uses this permission only to upload our own videos. It does not read, store, share or sell any other data. The sign-in tokens are stored privately and encrypted on the owner’s own devices and storage, and are never shared with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account’s security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or in TikTok’s settings under “Manage app permissions”.

Contact

Send a direct message to @find10daily on Instagram.
